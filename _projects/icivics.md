## The problem

iCivics teaches civics to millions of students, and many of its classroom readings were PDFs. A PDF is easy to print but hard to use on a screen: it's difficult to resize and difficult for screen readers, and it's a frustrating read for students who struggle with reading.

## Who it was for

Students who need readings that adapt to them, and the teachers trying to reach every student in a class.

## Process

I interviewed **10 stakeholders** and reviewed **5 competing platforms** to understand how readings were used in real classrooms and where students got stuck.
