## The problem

Many young people feel they spend more time on social media than they want to. The apps are designed to be hard to put down, and willpower alone rarely wins.

## Who it was for

Students and teens at NOMO, a pre-seed startup backed by Snapchat.

## Process

### Research

I surveyed more than 110 students about how they use social media, when it feels good, and when it feels compulsive.

{% include todo.html note="What did the survey show? Two or three findings, ideally with a number each." %}

### Design

From those findings, I designed rewards and streak mechanics and prototyped them.

{% include todo.html note="What did the rewards and streaks reward: time away from apps, intentional use, or something else?" %}

### Testing

In prototype tests, the new mechanics lifted click-through rate by **22%** and retention by **16%**.
